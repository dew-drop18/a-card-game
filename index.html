How to play

Reduce the rival commander from 30 health to 0 to claim the crown.

01

Gather supplies. Once per turn, choose food, wood, or stone to gather 2. Your stores carry over.

02

Play your hand. Cards cost a mix of supplies. Creatures join your company; spells resolve when played.

03

Take the field. Click a ready creature, then a rival creature or commander. A creature can attack once each turn.

04

Hold the line. Defense reduces damage received. Every strike deals at least 1 damage. New creatures must wait a turn before attacking.

Each empire has its own 100-card deck. An empty deck deals increasing fatigue damage instead of drawing.
